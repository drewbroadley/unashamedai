Get your iPhone onto QDLink

QDLink puts your phone’s screen on the C10’s centre display. An iPhone connects through Apple’s built-in Screen Mirroring, but first it has to join the car’s Wi-Fi, and QDLink doesn’t make the password easy to find.

The bit that isn’t obvious: the car’s Wi-Fi name and password are on QDLink’s help screens. Tap the ? in the top-right corner, then swipe across to the last help page, the one for iOS.

Good to know

  • What QDLink isLeapmotor’s phone-mirroring app on the C10, added by a software update because this generation of the car doesn’t have Apple CarPlay or Android Auto.
  • iPhones work tooSome reviews describe QDLink as Android-only. An iPhone connects with Screen Mirroring, which is already built into iOS, over the car’s own Wi-Fi.
  • Mostly a one-offYour iPhone remembers the car’s Wi-Fi after the first time, so next time you can jump straight to Screen Mirroring (step 6).
  • Do it parkedYou’ll be reading a password off the car screen and typing it into your phone.

Seven steps

On the car screen

  1. Open QDLink

    Open the app grid on the centre screen and tap QDLink.

  2. Tap the ? in the top-right corner

    QDLink opens on its connect screen. The ? in the top-right corner opens its help.

  3. Swipe to the last help page

    The help has several pages. Swipe across them until you reach the last one, which is the iOS page.

  4. Note the Wi-Fi name and password

    The iOS page shows the car’s Wi-Fi name and password. Leave it on screen, or write both down.

    Passwords are case-sensitive, so watch for look-alikes such as l and 1, or O and 0.

On your iPhone

  1. Join the car’s Wi-Fi

    Open Settings › Wi-Fi, tap the network named on the iOS help page, enter the password and tap Join.

  2. Start Screen Mirroring

    Open Control Center: swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen (or up from the bottom on an iPhone with a Home button). Tap Screen Mirroring, then pick the car from the list.

  3. Your iPhone is on the car screen

    Your iPhone’s screen now appears on the centre display. To stop, open Control Center again, tap Screen Mirroring and then Stop Mirroring.

    Names in these drawings are placeholders. Use the Wi-Fi name from the iOS help page, and whatever name the car shows in the Screen Mirroring list.

If it doesn’t work

The car isn’t in the Screen Mirroring list

Check your iPhone is on the car’s Wi-Fi (in Settings › Wi-Fi it should have a tick next to it) and that QDLink is open on the centre screen. Then open Screen Mirroring again.

The password won’t work

Go back to QDLink’s iOS help page and check it character by character. It’s case-sensitive, and some letters and numbers look alike.

I can’t find the iOS page

It’s the last page of the help, not the first. Keep swiping in the same direction until you can’t go any further.

Where this came from

Worked out on the site author’s own C10. There’s no video for this one; the hidden password on the help screen was the part that took a while to find.

This guide is unofficial. Leapmotor doesn’t make or support it, and a software update could move things around.
