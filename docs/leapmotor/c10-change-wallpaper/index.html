Put your own picture on the C10’s wallpaper

The C10 has no setting for using your own photo as the background. But if the AutoKit app is on your car’s screen, one of its settings opens the screen’s file browser, and that browser can pass any picture to the car’s own “set as wallpaper” tool. You need a USB stick and about two minutes.

There’s no undo. Once your picture is set, the only known way back to Leapmotor’s original wallpapers is to reset the screen system, which clears your other settings too. The owner who shared this method hadn’t found another way back as of May 2026. Choose a picture you’re happy to live with.

What you need

  • AutoKit on the car’s screenAutoKit is the app that runs Carlinkit CarPlay and Android Auto adapters, and some C10 owners have it installed. Look for it in the app grid. Without it this method doesn’t work, and this page doesn’t cover installing it.
  • A USB stick in the USB-A portThe stick the built-in dashcam records to is fine. If the dashcam can write to it, the screen can read it.
  • Landscape picturesThe screen is wide, so portrait photos get cropped hard. JPG and PNG both work.
  • Your phone disconnectedIf your phone connects, AutoKit goes straight into CarPlay or Android Auto and you can’t reach its settings. Turn off your phone’s Bluetooth or leave it outside the car.

Twelve steps

  1. Put your pictures on the USB stick

    On your computer, make a new folder on the stick (for example Wallpapers) and copy in the pictures you want.

    Leave the dashcam’s own folder alone.

  2. Open AutoKit

    Plug the stick into the car’s USB-A port, open the app grid and tap AutoKit.

  3. Tap the settings gear

    With no phone connected, AutoKit sits on its waiting-for-phone screen. Tap the gear in the top-left corner.

  4. Open Advanced settings

    Scroll down the settings list and tap Advanced settings.

  5. Find “OEM icon selection” and tap +

    Advanced settings runs to a second page. Scroll right to the bottom, find OEM icon selection and tap the + at the end of its row of icons.

    The + is meant for choosing an icon. You’re only using it because it opens the file browser, so the icon itself won’t change.

  6. Pick the USB stick

    An Open from panel slides in. Tap the USB stick, shown with a USB symbol and named LEAPMOTOR.

    Leapmotor C10 just above it is the car’s own storage, not the stick.

  7. Open your pictures folder

    Tap the folder you made in step 1.

    You’ll also see the dashcam folder, LOST.DIR and System Volume Information. Leave those alone.

  8. Open the picture with the expand icon

    Each file has small icons at the right-hand end of its row. On the picture you want, tap the one that looks a bit like a plus but is really four corners. That opens the picture full screen.

    Don’t tap the file name. That hands the picture back to AutoKit as an icon instead.

  9. Tap the three dots, then “Set as…”

    With the picture open, tap the three dots in the top-right corner and choose Set as…

  10. Choose Wallpaper

    A panel pops up from the bottom. Tap Wallpaper (it sits under the Gallery app). Not Contact photo.

  11. Check the crop and tap Save

    The screen shows how it will crop the picture. The dotted box is the crop it would use if the screen turned to portrait. The C10’s screen never turns, so only the wide landscape view matters. Tap Save.

    A “Setting wallpaper…” message flashes up while it works.

  12. Go home

    Tap the home icon in the bar along the bottom of the screen. Your picture is now the wallpaper.

    To try a different picture later, repeat steps 2 to 12.

If yours looks different

The menu names don’t quite match

The video this is based on was filmed on a car set to Spanish, so the names here are English translations. Your screen may word them slightly differently. The layout and the order of the steps should be the same.

There’s no gear icon in AutoKit

Your phone has probably connected and AutoKit has gone into CarPlay or Android Auto. Turn off your phone’s Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (or take it out of the car) and open AutoKit again.

The USB stick doesn’t appear in “Open from”

Make sure it’s in the USB-A (rectangular) port, not a USB-C charging port. If the dashcam records to the same stick, the screen can read it.

How do I get the original wallpaper back?

The only known way is to reset the car’s screen system, which also clears your other settings. If a gentler way turns up, it will go here.

Where this came from

A Telegram user called Wallas 630 worked this out, and Leapmotor C10 owner Juanlu de Toledo showed it step by step in a YouTube Short (in Spanish, 3 May 2026): Fondo de pantalla personalizado en Leapmotor C10. This page explains the same method in English, with drawn diagrams of each screen.

This guide is unofficial. Leapmotor doesn’t make or support it, and a software update could stop it working or put the original wallpaper back.
