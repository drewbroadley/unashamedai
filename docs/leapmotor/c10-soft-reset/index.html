Soft reset the C10’s screens

If the centre screen or the driver’s display freezes, lags or stops doing what it should, you can restart both without turning the car off. Hold two buttons on the steering wheel for 20 to 30 seconds and the screens switch off and start up again. Nothing is erased.

Pull over first if you can. This works while driving, and steering, brakes, drive controls and indicators keep working. But until the system is back up, the driver-assist safety features are off and the driver’s display is dark, so you’ve got no speedometer.

Good to know

  • When to use itThe centre screen is frozen or slow, the driver’s display looks wrong, or an app won’t behave. It’s the C10’s version of turning it off and on again.
  • What it restartsThe centre touchscreen and the driver’s display. The car stays on and in whatever gear it’s in.
  • What it doesn’t doIt’s a restart, not a factory reset. Your profiles, settings and saved data stay as they are.
  • Which way round“Left” and “right” mean the steering-wheel spokes as you see them from the driver’s seat. The diagrams show a right-hand-drive car, as sold in New Zealand.

Five steps

  1. Find the voice button

    On the right-hand spoke of the steering wheel, find the voice-command button. It’s the top-right button on that control pad, marked with sound waves.

  2. Find the left scroll wheel

    On the left-hand spoke, find the scroll wheel in the middle of the control pad, between the ‹ and › marks. As well as rolling, it presses in like a button.

  3. Hold both for 20 to 30 seconds

    Press and hold the voice button. While you’re holding it, push the left scroll wheel in and hold that too. Keep holding both.

    Nothing seems to happen for a while. That’s normal, so keep holding.

  4. Let go when both screens go black

    The centre screen and the driver’s display both switch off. That means the restart has started, so you can let go.

  5. Wait for it to start up

    The driver’s display comes back first. The centre screen shows the Leapmotor logo for a little longer before the home screen returns. Allow up to a minute.

    If the problem’s still there after a restart, it’s worth telling your dealer.

If it doesn’t work

I held both and nothing happened

Check you’re pushing the left scroll wheel in, not rolling it, and that you’re still holding the voice button at the same time. Both need to stay held together for the full 20 to 30 seconds, so start counting again from when you’ve got both down.

The voice assistant popped up

Pressing the voice button on its own starts the voice assistant, so this can happen before you get the scroll wheel in. Keep holding the voice button, push the scroll wheel in, and keep both held until the screens go black.

Will this undo a custom wallpaper?

Not known. The owner who shared the custom wallpaper method said getting the original back takes “a reset” without saying which kind. A soft reset is quick and harmless, so it’s worth trying first.
