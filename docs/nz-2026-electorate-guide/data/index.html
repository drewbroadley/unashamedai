Open Data & Source Files

Everything behind the interactive split-vote guide is published here as spreadsheets (CSV), structured JSON, and readable Markdown under CC0 1.0 Public Domain.

Spreadsheet · CSV candidates.csv All 479 candidates across all 71 electorates with party, Vote.nz list rank, MMP entry path, career background, 2023–26 record, and 2026 priorities. Opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Spreadsheet · CSV electorates-and-suburbs.csv All 232 city, town, and suburb groups mapped to their official 2026 General or Māori electorate under the August 2025 Representation Commission Final Report. Spreadsheet · CSV parties-and-policies.csv All 7 major political parties compared across 8 policy domains, including 2017–2026 delivery scores, 2023–26 actions, and 2026–29 manifesto commitments. Structured · JSON electorates.json Full structured JSON for all 71 electorates, their 2025/2026 suburb boundaries, local issue clashes vs complementarities, and all 479 candidate profiles. Structured · JSON parties.json Full structured JSON for all 7 political parties, their Promised vs Performed delivery records, and stances across all 8 policy domains. Reference · Markdown by-electorate.md Human-readable Markdown directory of all 71 electorates, their 2026 suburb coverage, and candidate summary tables.
